Spineless Zani — polygon silhouette animation demo

Loads Zani's full sprite (webp, alpha channel), traces the alpha boundary with Moore-neighbourhood walking, simplifies with Douglas–Peucker, and renders the clipped polygon through an SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter — real organic pixel warping, not a transform. Sub-pixel parallax drift and Spectro particles on top. No rigging data — just pixels in, pseudo-spine out.
