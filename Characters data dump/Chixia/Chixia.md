## Review

**Ratings**: DPS — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA Value),
T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: ranged abilities hit moving/flying targets easily while staying at distance; fundamentally
simple to understand and play; works as both Main and Dual DPS (notably with Changli); free.

**Cons**: very little AoE potential; difficult to parry with (few options); tied for highest Ultimate
Energy cost among damage dealers; subpar damage vs top-tier DPS; no dedicated support option; timing
Boom Boom after exactly 30 bullets is tricky; her best damaging combos take noticeable field time;
vulnerable to interruption while channeling her Forte skill (large damage loss if interrupted).

**Summary**: a free 4★ Fusion Pistol DPS with one of the hardest-hitting Ultimates in the game, paired
with a unique run'n'gun channeled Forte. Her Forte (Thermobaric Bullets, cap 60) is spent via DAKA
DAKA!, finished by Boom Boom after 30+ bullets — this plus her Ultimate (one of the highest multipliers
in the game) make up the bulk of her damage. Awkward meta position: designed as a DPS but doesn't want
extended field time, so she's best as a Dual DPS — her clear-cut partner is Changli (massive Outro buff,
best swap-cancel windows in the game), letting a Chixia team clear the Tower comfortably. Problems:
near-impossible to parry (gun class), little AoE beyond her Ultimate, costly Ultimate Energy-wise,
difficult as a hypercarry (lacking good Skill/Liberation buffers outside limited 5★s used better
elsewhere), Inferno Rider needs pre-rotation placement, and Forte-channel interruption is common.
Not a perfect character, but comfortably clears most content in the right context with proper
teammates and build.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Warm-Up**: use Resonance Skill on cooldown outside the burst window to build Resonance Energy and
Forte — aim for 2 casts, saving 1 charge for the burst combo (unless S4, which allows more flexibility).
Swap-cancel each Warm-Up Skill cast immediately.

**Burst Combo**: Echo (before combo) → Intro → Skill (consume Forte from Warm-Up, repeat to the
indicator) → Basic Attack (fires Boom Boom, ~4s full execution, movement allowed but must complete for
full value) → Ultimate (right after a full Forte channel, since it grants an ATK boost) → another full
Forte channel into Boom Boom → Outro. Landing abilities matters more than the exact sequence — free to
adapt if it avoids damage loss.

**Synergies**: Brant (perfect partner — contributes real damage while amplifying Chixia's Fusion+Skill
DMG by 20%/25%). Changli (one of her highest-damaging partners, Dual DPS playstyle, 20% Fusion/25%
Liberation DMG Amp Outro). Lupa (one of her best buffers via Fusion DMG Amp; best paired with a Basic
Attack-scaling third Fusion DPS like Encore or ideally Brant — "Chixia's newest best team archetype").
Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports, slot into any team).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Brant Dual DPS Team, Changli Team — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text.
