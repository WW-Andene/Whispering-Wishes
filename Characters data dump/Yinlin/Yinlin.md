## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: Stringmaster is one of the strongest weapons in the game and improves her damage
significantly; benefits a lot from swap cancelling while retaining a good baseline without it; very
good AoE damage; strong Liberation DMG and Electro DMG buffs to Electro DPS partners.

**Cons**: can be outshined by other units in meta teams (most notably Zhezhi in a Jinhsi team); gains a
lot of consistency from swap/animation cancelling, uncomfortable for casual players; can be outshined
by quickswap Dual DPS options in certain teams; unusually high reliance on her signature weapon.

**Summary**: a 5★ Electro Rectifier Hybrid built around big AoE damage, even off-field, in short field
windows. Near-mandatory alongside a big Main DPS carry — best synergies are Xiangli Yao and Calcharo
(both amplified massively by her 20% Electro/25% Liberation DMG Amp Outro), and she's the strong
second-best choice (behind Zhezhi) for Jinhsi teams. Always run Moonlit Clouds as a Hybrid — don't run
her as a Main DPS with Void Thunder, she underperforms drastically outside her Hybrid role. Relies
heavily on her signature Stringmaster to reach full strength. Considered a very strong contender among
top Hybrids, especially valuable for Xiangli Yao/Calcharo/Jinhsi owners.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Rotation**: Intro → Basic P4 (optionally swap cancel) → Skill: Magnetic Roar → Heavy Attack
→ Skill: Lightning Execution (try to cancel Heavy ATK endlag) → Liberation (cast right as Yinlin lands)
→ Basic P1 → Forte Heavy: Chameleon Cipher → Echo (swap cancel, best right after Chameleon Cipher) →
Outro.

**Synergies**: Iuno (best synergy nowadays — close second to Ciaccona for buffing Iuno's Liberation DMG
via Outro). Jinhsi (a competitive pair to Zhezhi, slightly worse, but still one of the best teams in
the game). Xiangli Yao (extremely important 20% Electro/25% Liberation DMG Amp Outro buffs him to a top
DPS). Augusta (worse option than Mortefi — only worth slotting if Mortefi is taken or Yinlin is
preferred). Calcharo (natural pairing, similar to Xiangli Yao, plus many mutual Swap Cancel windows).
Youhu (buffs Yinlin the most of any partner, thanks to her high Coordinated Attack personal damage and
his 100% Coordinated Attack DMG Amp Outro). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports that can
slot into any team).

**Example Teams**: Main DPS Iuno, Jinhsi Team, Electro Teams — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text.

No dump file existed before this pass — created. `bestWeapon`, `bestEchoes`, `weaponAlts`, `teams`, the

CHAR_BUFF_TABLE's outro/Pain Immersion values, and RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA's S1/S2/S4/S5/S6 values all

trigger — an already well-audited piece of this file from a prior pass).

**Base stats**: confirmed exact match at Lv.90 (HP 11000, ATK 400, DEF 1283, Max Energy 125) — an
earlier pass of this dump misread the extracted text's "Lv." and "90" as separate lines and
mis-transcribed them as "Lv. 79" with correspondingly wrong HP/ATK/DEF figures; this was an extraction
error on this dump's part, not a real source discrepancy (the user caught and corrected it 2026-09-03).
No app data was ever changed based on the bad read.

**Real bugs found and fixed (2026-09-03)**:
1. `RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA['Yinlin'].s3` and `yinlin.blocks.js`'s `yinlin.chain.s3-unyielding-verdict`
   block both modeled Judgment Strike's own DMG Multiplier as `skillDmg`, but Judgment Strike is
   explicitly a **Coordinated Attack** — confirmed by the source's own text ("Yinlin having the highest
   personal damage of any Coordinated Attack DPS", "trigger additional Coordinated Attacks") and
   already correctly reflected in `yinlin.coordatk.judgement-strike`'s `coordDmg` categorization. The
   old `skillDmg` value was a dead buff for its real intended target while silently over-crediting
   Magnetic Roar/Lightning Execution/Furious Thunder (her actual skillDmg-categorized moves). Fixed to
   `coordDmg` in both files; also corrected `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS['Yinlin']`'s Judgment Strike row note,
   which self-contradicted ("Coordinated ATK triggered... considered Skill DMG").
2. Found while fixing (1): `calcEngine.js`'s `applyResonanceChain()` (the legacy engine's main-DPS
   branch) had no handler for a `coordDmg` Resonance Chain stat at all — it would have silently dropped
   S3's contribution for any character once corrected. Added the missing branch.
3. Inherent Skill **Deadly Focus** (Lightning Execution DMG+10% vs Sinner's-Marked targets, and self
   ATK+10% for 4s when triggered) was entirely missing from both `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Yinlin']` and
   `yinlin.blocks.js`. Added: the ATK+10%/4s component to both files (safe, generic self-stat, no
   over-crediting risk); the Lightning Execution-specific DMG+10% only to `yinlin.blocks.js`, scoped via
   `scopedToBlockId` to `yinlin.skill.lightning-execution` specifically, since Yinlin's modeled rotation
   always has Sinner's Mark up by the time Lightning Execution casts (applied by Intro/Thundering
   Wrath) — left out of the legacy flat `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE` since that table has no scoping mechanism and
   would otherwise over-credit Magnetic Roar/Furious Thunder.

4 tests updated (parity assertions rebalanced for the corrected S3 category and the new Deadly Focus
blocks), full suite green (1337/1337).
