## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: high mobility, can deal damage while moving; powerful Resonance Skill DMG Amp Outro to
support the team; gains further support tools with Sequences; mixes ranged/melee for extra flexibility.

**Cons**: long Concerto rotation, higher field time than other Hybrid/support characters; can be very
Energy-hungry, needing teammates that provide Energy plus good personal ER.

**Summary**: a 4★ Electro Broadblade Hybrid buffer (dedicated DPS play is possible for committed
players). Her Outro amplifies Resonance Skill DMG — a damage type "currently under-serviced" among
Hybrids (previously only Taoqi). Two goals when played as Hybrid: fast Concerto generation for the
Outro, and enough Resonance Energy for her Ultimate. Her kit centers on a dual Forte Gauge (Yellow
Light Spark/Red Light Spark), the first of its kind — filling either half via attacks in that stance,
then spending it via an empowered Resonance Skill that swaps stances and buffs the following Basic
Attacks. Simple to execute at a basic level. Best suited to teams with a Skill-damage-focused Main DPS
and mobility-friendly rotations. At release, lacked an ideal team/teammate for her Hybrid role, needed
significant Resonance Energy help, and had a longer-than-average field time for the role — landing
without a best-in-slot position in any endgame meta team at release.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation** (long for a Hybrid — swap-cancel to shorten): Intro → Liberation → Skill: Energized Pounce
(optional swap cancel and immediately back) → Red Spotlight: Basic Attack 1-3 (optional swap cancel and
immediately back after hit 3) → Skill: Energized Rebound → Yellow Light: Basic Attack → Channelled
Dash: Glare×6 → Skill: Energized Pounce → Echo (swap cancel) → Outro.

**Synergies**: Jinhsi (significantly buffs her nukes via 38% Resonance Skill DMG Amp Outro; note —
Shorekeeper does NOT work in this team since Jinhsi's Forte needs Coordinated Attacks, which neither
Lumi nor Shorekeeper provide, unlike Verina). Carlotta (near-exclusively Skill DMG, natural synergy with
Lumi's Outro — but timing her Outro right as Carlotta's Forte nuke activates is tricky, making the team
awkward to play). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Jinhsi), Best Team (Carlotta) — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text.
